Hand Gesture Rules (0 ≤ X ≤ 9)

As specified in the competition guidelines, fingers should be held facing the camera:

0 : Closed fist (0 extended fingers)
1 to 5 : 1 to 5 fingers extended on one hand (Thumb, Index, Middle, Ring, Pinky)
6 to 9 : Open hand (5 fingers on one hand) + 1 to 4 fingers on the second hand
